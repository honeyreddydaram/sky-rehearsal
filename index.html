    Bring your flight profile

    Import altitude and true airspeed schedules from your instructor or a prepared flight recording.

    Use route progress 0–1, altitude in feet with an explicit reference, and true airspeed in knots. MSL heights also need a local geoid offset to position them in Cesium. Imported data remains on this browser; it does not certify the route.

    Optional callouts can include your instructor’s turn and descent instructions. The example file contains demo values, not an actual flight profile.
