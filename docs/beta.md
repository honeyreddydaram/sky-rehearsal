# Private beta protocol

The engineering milestones do not demonstrate learning effectiveness. No real beta
results have been collected or invented in this repository.

Recruit through the product owner: 5–10 consenting trainee pilots, 2–3 instructors
and one flight school. Contact details and enrollment decisions are external to
this code task. Use instructor-reviewed airport/circuit data and applicable aircraft
inputs; explain the ground-based, simplified and unverified-data limitations.

For each trainee:

1. Record prior circuit familiarity and a baseline landmark/sequence assessment.
2. Assign a consistent airport/runway/difficulty exercise. Record help used and
   device/browser; distinguish simulated metrics from measured pilot performance.
3. Ask the trainee to rehearse and submit an attempt. Let an instructor review it
   and, if consented, an explicitly shared real recording.
4. Repeat a comparable assessment with different question order and a delayed
   retention check. Keep baseline and follow-up conditions comparable.
5. Collect instructor notes on debrief usefulness, incorrect guidance/data, confusing
   controls and the time needed to review an attempt.

Use the local aggregate metrics export and authorized school submissions as supporting
data, not as certified scores. Compare within-student changes and report the sample
size, missing measurements, hints, repeated-exposure effects and limitations. Keep
identifying participant keys separately from exported aggregate data.

Decide launch criteria with the school before the trial: no unresolved access/privacy
failure, accepted airport-data corrections, usable tablet controls, successful backup
recovery, instructor-accepted debrief explanations and a predefined improvement/
retention target. A small beta provides directional evidence, not a certification or
causal efficacy claim.

Maintain a private worksheet with participant code, consent, baseline, follow-up,
retention check, instructor review time, issues and withdrawal/deletion requests.
Review raw recordings only under the agreed sharing policy. Keep credentials and
participant files out of Git. Conclude with a written go/no-go decision and an
explicit list of remaining production and instructional validation work.
